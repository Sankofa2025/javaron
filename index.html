The Sankofa Principle

Learning from what came before to protect what comes next.

In the Akan tradition, Sankofa is commonly represented by a bird looking back while moving forward. The symbol expresses the idea that wisdom from the past can help shape a stronger future.

That principle informs our approach to legal work. In criminal defense, we examine the facts, procedures, and decisions that led to an accusation. In civil litigation, we look closely at the origin of a dispute, the governing agreements, and the available remedies.

Every legal matter is different. Our role is to understand yours fully, explain your options clearly, and help you make informed decisions about the road ahead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you contact the firm.

Does contacting the firm create an attorney-client relationship?

No. Contacting the firm through this website, by phone, or by email does not create an attorney-client relationship. Representation begins only after the firm has completed any required conflict review and both you and the firm enter into a written engagement agreement.

What should I include in my initial message?

Provide basic contact details, your general legal need, and any non-confidential timing concern. Do not send documents, admissions, detailed facts, account numbers, or other sensitive information until the firm confirms that it can consider representation.

What if I have an urgent criminal-defense issue?

Call the criminal-defense emergency line directly at (808) 927-1621. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a medical emergency, call 911. This website is not a substitute for emergency services.

Does the firm guarantee results?

No. Legal outcomes depend on the facts, law, evidence, court, opposing parties, and other circumstances unique to each matter. Past outcomes do not guarantee a similar result in a future case.
