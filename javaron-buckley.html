About Javaron

As the Founding Attorney of Sankofa Law Group, Javaron Buckley represents clients in personal injury and criminal defense matters with an emphasis on meticulous preparation, clear communication, and individualized strategy. He understands that legal problems are often defining moments in a person’s life, and he believes that every client deserves counsel who treats them with the utmost seriousness, dignity, and respect.

Guided by the firm’s core mission to Teach, Defend, and Unify, Javaron takes a holistic approach to legal representation. Whether he is negotiating a complex personal injury settlement or building a rigorous defense against criminal charges, he leaves no stone unturned. His track record includes successfully settling millions in personal injury cases and fiercely protecting the rights of individuals facing state and federal allegations.

Practice Areas

  • Criminal Defense: State and federal felony defense, DUI and traffic-related offenses, white-collar and financial-crime matters, and expungement/record-sealing.
  • Personal Injury: Auto and trucking accidents, slip and fall/premises liability, wrongful death claims, and catastrophic or severe injury cases.
